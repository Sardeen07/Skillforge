list_orders in api.py backs GET /orders. A big customer has 80,000 orders and the endpoint now times out and uses gigabytes of memory. Change it to list_orders(store, customer_id, limit=None, cursor=None) returning {"items": [...], "next_cursor": str or None}: pages of 50 by default, never more than 100 per page even if the client asks, oldest first by id, with next_cursor None on the last page.
